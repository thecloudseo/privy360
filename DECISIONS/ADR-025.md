# ADR-025: Customer VPC deployment

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Customer-owned infrastructure and private endpoints with portable adapters, customer key/DNS/identity/telemetry.

## Alternatives and rationale

Mandatory operator cloud services create residency/connectivity lock-in.

## Consequences and verification

Provider conformance tests, customer operations ownership and scoped support. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../DEPLOYMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
