# ADR-017: Secrets management

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Portable Vault/customer secret-store interface, workload identity and short-lived credentials; DB stores references only.

## Alternatives and rationale

Environment files/repository/database plaintext secrets leak across logs/backups.

## Consequences and verification

Provider selection security approval OPEN-02; rotation and incident revocation tested. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
