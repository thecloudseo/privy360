# ADR-031: Workflow and approval integrity

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Explicit state machines, version-bound maker/checker approvals, durable work IDs and safe retry/reconciliation.

## Alternatives and rationale

Editable status strings or blind external retries create false completion and unsafe legal actions.

## Consequences and verification

No self-approval fallback; unknown timers/side effects remain visible, not guessed. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../WORKFLOW_SPEC.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
