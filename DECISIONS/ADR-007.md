# ADR-007: Keycloak identity

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Keycloak brokers OIDC/SAML and AD/LDAP; local mappings and deterministic permissions remain application-owned.

## Alternatives and rationale

Custom password/SSO implementation increases risk; IdP roles alone cannot express object ABAC.

## Consequences and verification

Separate staff/principal/service clients and deployment realms; mandatory staff MFA. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
