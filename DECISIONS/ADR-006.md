# ADR-006: Temporal durable workflows

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Temporal executes long-running rights/deletion/notification/integration workflows through domain activities.

## Alternatives and rationale

Ad hoc cron/status polling lacks durable retries and versioned execution; Temporal history alone cannot be business truth.

## Consequences and verification

No secrets/PII in history; idempotent activities, versioned workers and replay tests. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../WORKFLOW_SPEC.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
