# ADR-030: Privacy minimisation and lifecycle

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Store metadata/opaque principal refs; purpose-specific sensitive fields, approved retention, holds and tracked downstream erasure.

## Alternatives and rationale

Copying customer data estates increases breach scope; blanket immutable retention conflicts with minimization.

## Consequences and verification

OPEN-04/05 retention/identity assurance review; preserve minimal legal evidence rather than raw PII. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../DATABASE_SPEC.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
