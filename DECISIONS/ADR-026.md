# ADR-026: Government and disconnected deployment

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Customer Kubernetes/private infrastructure with local dependencies and signed offline update/corpus bundles.

## Alternatives and rationale

Public SaaS-only identity, CDN, telemetry or AI prevents restricted-network deployment.

## Consequences and verification

Customer CA/DNS/HSM/SIEM/backup integration; independent government production gate. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../DEPLOYMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
