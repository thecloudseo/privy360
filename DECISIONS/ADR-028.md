# ADR-028: Regulatory source ingestion

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Allowlisted official-source or signed offline intake→checksum/provenance→parse draft→human/legal review→atomic signed publication.

## Alternatives and rationale

Scraping/model memory cannot establish authoritative law; source change cannot auto-change customer obligations.

## Consequences and verification

Official corpus absent; legal gate OPEN-01. Impact analysis proposes reviewed tenant changes. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../DOMAIN_MODEL.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
