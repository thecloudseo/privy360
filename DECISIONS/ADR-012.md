# ADR-012: Transactional audit and integrity

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Business mutation+audit+outbox atomic; append-only per-tenant stream with hash chain and independent signed WORM anchors.

## Alternatives and rationale

Async best-effort audit can disappear; DB-only hash chain cannot resist privileged rewrite.

## Consequences and verification

Audit failure aborts mutation; retention/minimization and verification separate from operational logs. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../EVENT_MODEL.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
