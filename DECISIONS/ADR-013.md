# ADR-013: Versioned regulatory data

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Stable legal IDs plus immutable effective/recorded-time versions, approved citations and reviewed applicability.

## Alternatives and rationale

Hard-coded law and overwriting text destroy traceability and multi-jurisdiction support.

## Consequences and verification

No authoritative legal seed until sources approved; examples are not law. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../DOMAIN_MODEL.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
