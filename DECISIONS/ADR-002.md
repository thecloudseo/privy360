# ADR-002: Next.js and React surfaces

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Separate enterprise console and Privacy Centre Next.js/React apps with server BFF sessions.

## Alternatives and rationale

A single public/admin surface increases session/content blast radius; browser-only tokens increase theft exposure.

## Consequences and verification

No business DB access in frontend; accessible/i18n design system shared. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../API_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
