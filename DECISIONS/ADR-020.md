# ADR-020: AI Gateway containment

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

AI disabled by default; deterministic scoped retrieval/tool registry, minimization, approved providers and human-gated proposals.

## Alternatives and rationale

Direct model database access or model authorization decisions create unbounded leakage/escalation.

## Consequences and verification

OPEN-07 provider approval; no automatic legal decisions, rights rejection, breach submission or destructive execution. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../SECURITY_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
