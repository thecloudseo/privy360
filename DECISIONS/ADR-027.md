# ADR-027: Backup and disaster recovery

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Encrypted immutable separate-plane backups, coordinated restore manifests, erasure reconciliation and tested recovery.

## Alternatives and rationale

Untested backups do not establish recoverability; restoring old personal data violates lifecycle intent.

## Consequences and verification

OPEN-03/06 residency and contractual RPO/RTO approval; repeat drills before promises. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../DEPLOYMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
