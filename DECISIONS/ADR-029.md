# ADR-029: Full-product baseline and bid sequencing

Date: 2026-09-30

Status: Selected for architecture baseline

## Context

Privy360 must implement the full MASTER_SPEC platform across four deployment modes with default-deny security and durable traceability. The bid is an early release milestone only.

## Decision

Full platform contracts apply from day one; early bid contains permanent production-quality vertical slices only.

## Alternatives and rationale

Disposable demo schemas/auth shortcuts cause rework and contradict complete-product mission.

## Consequences and verification

Basic vendors/risk/incidents/reporting enter bid; advanced workflows later; release gates remain intact. Normative details and required tests: [contract](../BUILD_PLAN.md). Applicable approval gates are in [ARCHITECTURE](../ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Revisit trigger

Measured failure to meet security, portability, throughput or regulatory traceability requirements; propose a superseding ADR with migration and compatibility impact before changing this choice.
